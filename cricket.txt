Under the golden sun’s warm light,
Eleven hearts step out to fight.
A bat held firm, a bowler’s glare,
Tension humming in the air.

The leather sphere cuts through the breeze,
A perfect swing with practiced ease.
Crack! — the willow sings its song,
As cheers erupt both loud and strong.

From Mumbai streets to London’s ground,
Cricket’s spirit knows no bound.
A child with dreams, a dusty pitch,
Where heroes rise from humble pitch.

Then came Dhoni — Captain Cool, calm as night,
Turning pressure into guiding light.
Helicopter shots across the sky,
Teaching dreamers how to fly. 

Gloves of steel, a mind so sharp,
Finishing games like crafted art.
“Dhoni finishes off in style…” still remains,
An echo dancing through cricket’s veins.

The spinner weaves a clever spell,
The batter reads it, judges well.
A yorker strikes like lightning’s art,
A bouncer tests the bravest heart.

Not just a game of runs and fame,
But stories carved in passion’s flame.
From Sachin’s grace to Dhoni’s reign,
Cricket makes legends live again.
